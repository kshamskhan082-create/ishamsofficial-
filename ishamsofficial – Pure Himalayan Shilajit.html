Questions

How do I use the resin?

Dissolve a pea-sized amount (about 300 mg) in warm water or milk once a day. Start with less and see how you feel.

How long does delivery take?

Usually 3 to 5 working days across Pakistan. We confirm every order on WhatsApp first.

Is shilajit a medicine?

No. It is a traditional supplement and does not treat or cure any disease. Ask your doctor first if you are pregnant, on medication or have a health condition.
